Weekly parenting routine

Make the week clear before it starts.

Each parent submits an independent acknowledgement for the same week. The form creates a clear written record without turning routine parenting into a negotiation.

Private by design
Only include information needed to organise the week. Do not enter passwords, relationship disputes or sensitive private details.

How it works

  1. 01
    Choose the weekUse the Monday that starts the relevant week.
  2. 02
    Confirm the routineRecord overnights, handovers, calls and key commitments.
  3. 03
    Submit separatelyEach parent uses the same week reference.
Working principle
If a change is not confirmed in writing, the agreed routine remains in place.
